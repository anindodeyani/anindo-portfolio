👋 Who I Am

Hi, I’m Anindo, a 3D Artist focused on clean modeling, realistic lighting, and minimal presentation. I love creating detailed 3D works that look simple but feel premium.

I’m also studying Computer Science, which helps me think logically and build structured, organized projects.

🚀 My Journey

My journey in 3D was very amazing. I did an internship in 3D in the last semester of my diploma degree. From there, my 3D journey started. After that, I got a job in that office right after the internship. That office was my first office. Its name is Right River Bangladesh Limited.

This is how my journey in 3D began. Then, to fulfill my childhood dream, I enrolled in Computer Science. I am currently studying in my fourth year of Computer Science.

My goal is to combine 3D visualization and development to build premium digital experiences that feel modern and smooth.
